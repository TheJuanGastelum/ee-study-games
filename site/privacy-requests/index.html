Your data and privacy requests

1. Data saved in this browser

This is the only personal-ish data the games keep (progress and settings). It lives on your device. Use these buttons to see it or erase it.

Deleting resets your levels and stars on this device and also forgets that you dismissed the privacy notice. Saved data is also deleted automatically after 12 months without a visit.

2. Ask the operator about data they hold

The operator holds almost nothing about you. The only things are hosting logs kept by the host and any earlier request you sent. You can ask to access or delete these (your “right to be forgotten”), or send any other privacy question. We reply within 30 days.

The form collects only what is needed to answer you: an email address, the request type and optional details. It is received by Netlify, the host. Do not include passwords or sensitive information.
